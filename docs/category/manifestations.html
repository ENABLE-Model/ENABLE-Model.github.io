Manifestations

A comprehensive list of real-world organizations, tools, and actions that exemplify both proactive care and reactive compensations related to accessibility. They provide concrete scenarios that can serve as conduits for understanding accessibility in practice. If a public entity has done something that aligns with the ENABLE model, we'll notice.

📄️ 2gether-International

Disabled founders join 2Gether-International's accelerator to secure mentorship and investor interest that bakes accessibility into new startups.

📄️ Access Trax

Wheelchair users lay down Access Trax fold-up mats so they can cross sand or grass when public paths have no ramps.

📄️ Accessible EDU Consulting

Campus teams hire Accessible EDU to audit and fix course sites before the semester, preventing inaccessible syllabi from reaching students.

📄️ AccessNow

Travellers check the AccessNow map to pick restaurants and hotels that other disabled users have flagged as accessible.

📄️ AgrAbility

Farmers with disabilities contact AgrAbility to retrofit tractors and tools after conventional equipment locks them out.

📄️ All Access Life

Viewers watch All Access Life product videos to decide which adaptive gadgets can replace inaccessible mainstream tech.

📄️ America's Warrior Partnership

Disabled veterans use AWP's local coordinators to navigate benefit systems that online portals make inaccessible.

📄️ Angel City Sports

Athletes join Angel City meets to access adaptive equipment and coaching when mainstream leagues lack para-divisions.

📄️ Apple

- Users enable VoiceOver, Display Zoom, or Switch Control in iOS/macOS to work around apps that ignore accessibility guidelines.

📄️ Aspiritech

- Software companies contract Aspiritech's autistic QA teams to find a11y bugs before shipping.

📄️ AT4D

Policymakers and NGO staff cite AT4D research when drafting national assistive-technology strategies before funds are released.

📄️ Use Be My Eyes

Blind and low-vision users open the app to get a human volunteer or AI description when a screen, label, or CAPTCHA is unreadable.

📄️ Beacon College

Faculty download Beacon guidelines and adjust course materials before term start so students with learning differences aren't excluded.

📄️ Benetech / Bookshare

Readers with print disabilities log in to Bookshare to obtain DAISY, Braille, or MP3 editions when the standard print book is unusable.

📄️ BraunAbility

Wheelchair users buy or rent BraunAbility vans to travel independently when transit systems or rideshares lack ramped vehicles

📄️ Bri Scalesse

Fashion students and brands follow Bri Scalesse's campaigns to design runway shows and ad shoots that include wheelchair models from the start.

📄️ Cephable

Game and app developers integrate Cephable's SDK so future users can control interfaces with head movements or voice instead of a mouse.

📄️ Circleg

Amputees in low-resource regions assemble Circleg's low-cost prosthetic kits when hospital-grade limbs are unaffordable or unavailable.

📄️ Deque / axe-core

QA engineers run the open-source axe-core library in CI pipelines to automatically block builds that introduce new WCAG violations.

📄️ Disability Culture Lab

- Editors commission essays from DCL to replace deficit-focused stories before publishing disability coverage.

📄️ Diversability

Disabled professionals join Diversability's online community and events to trade advice and peer-mentor around barriers in mainstream networking spaces.

📄️ Docs with Disabilities Initiative

Medical schools assign the Docs With Disabilities podcast to faculty so curricula are updated with first-person disability perspectives before new courses launch.

📄️ Disability Rights Education & Defense Fund (DREDF)

- People contact DREDF attorneys to file ADA and Section 504 lawsuits after digital or physical access is denied.

📄️ Educause

Higher education institutions and IT professionals leverage Educause's resources and community to advance technology, including accessibility, within academic environments.

📄️ Embla Medical

Amputee athletes buy Embla's running blades to compete when public sports programs don't supply adaptive gear.

📄️ Enable Ventures

A disability-focused venture fund that bakes accessibility and inclusive hiring into term sheets.

📄️ European Commission

Accessibility advocates cite policies led by Inmaculada Placencia Porrero to make EU projects adopt built-environment and ICT accessibility standards before funding.

📄️ Everway

School districts deploy Everway's IEP-management suite when legacy SIS platforms exclude disabled students' accommodations.

📄️ Fable

Product teams hire disabled testers on Fable to run usability sessions before shipping new features.

📄️ GAAD Foundation

Teams schedule GAAD “bug-bash” days each May to remove accessibility blockers before the next release.

📄️ Global Alliance of Assistive Technology Organizations (GAATO)

National ministries reference GAATO briefs to set assistive-tech procurement rules before tenders open.

📄️ Gallaudet University

Ed-tech developers license Gallaudet's bilingual ASL-English story apps so children get accessible literacy material at release.

📄️ Google

Internal and third-party teams follow Google's “Products for All” guidance to embed accessible APIs during design, not after launch.

📄️ Hopper

Recreational runners purchase Hopper's low-cost carbon-fiber blade when public health insurance won't cover mainstream sports prostheses.

📄️ Howe Innovation Center

Disability-tech founders attend Howe hackathons to refine product-market fit with disabled users before raising capital.

📄️ Humanware

Blind users buy HumanWare braille displays and GPS devices when mass-market phones lack tactile feedback.

📄️ International Foundation for Electoral Systems (IFES)

Election commissions use IFES toolkits to add tactile ballots and accessible polling sites before voting day.

📄️ inABLE

Kenyan schools host inABLE computer labs so blind learners gain digital skills alongside sighted peers instead of waiting for post-hoc fixes.

📄️ Infinite Access

Companies contract Infinite Access to audit design systems and coach dev teams before inaccessible components reach production.

📄️ Innovating Inclusion

Employers book Shani Dhanda workshops to rewrite hiring policies and vendor criteria around disability inclusion.

📄️ Inventivio / Tactonom

Blind students use Inventivio's Tactonom Reader to explore tactile graphics when textbooks lack embossed diagrams.

📄️ Irisbond

People with motor disabilities mount Irisbond's Hiru eye-tracker to type and speak when keyboards and speech are impossible.

📄️ Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts

Patrons choose Kennedy Center performances with captioning, audio description, and relaxed-sensory formats curated by the Kennedy Center's Office of Accessibility.

📄️ KITE Research Institute

Rehab engineers use KITE's gait-lab data to design safer mobility devices ahead of clinical trials.

📄️ Koalaa

Amputees order Koalaa's soft “sock-style” arms online when clinical prosthetic services are too costly or unavailable.

📄️ Lachi / RAMPD

Concert bookers download RAMPD's accessibility rider so venues add ramps, captions, and ASL before the show.

📄️ Lainey Feingold

Disabled customers hire Lainey Feingold to negotiate “Structured Negotiation” agreements after digital services fail WCAG.

📄️ Landscape Structures

City planners specify Landscape Structures' ramped decks and sensory panels so new playgrounds launch inclusive from day one.

📄️ Making Space

Studios use Making Space guidelines to hire and accommodate disabled talent in pre-production, not as an afterthought.

📄️ Meta Platforms

When content creators don't provide alt text for their images, Meta's AI adds auto-generated alt-text so that blind Facebook and Instagram users can use screen-reader software to receive some sort of text descriptions at post time.

📄️ Microsoft

Enterprise buyers embed Microsoft's supplier clause that requires all deliverables to conform to WCAG AA before contracts are signed.

📄️ Minds of all Kinds

Neurodivergent adults attend MoAK's sensory-friendly events when typical nightlife overwhelms them.

📄️ Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

Prosthetic startups adapt Hugh Herr's BiOM powered-ankle research to design next-gen limbs ahead of FDA submission.

📄️ Mobility International USA

Study-abroad coordinators use MIUSA checklists to add accessibility clauses to partner-school agreements.

📄️ Move United

Athletes join Move United chapters to access sit-skiing, archery, and grants when local leagues lack inclusive gear.

📄️ National Down Syndrome Society

Self-advocates use NDSS toolkits and Kayla McKeon's briefings to lobby lawmakers after services exclude people with Down syndrome.

📄️ Navilens

Blind transit riders scan NaviLens codes to hear platform info when signage is unreadable.

📄️ NBCUniversal Media

Producers add captions and extended audio description to every Olympic livestream before broadcast, following NBCU's 2024 accessibility plan.

📄️ New Disabled South

Southerners with disabilities cite NDS research to campaign for Medicaid expansion and voting-access bills.

📄️ Nike

Designers study Nike's hands-free FlyEase mechanisms to build shoes that users can step into when tying laces is a barrier.

📄️ NV Access

Windows users install the free, open-source NVDA screen reader to navigate apps that ship without semantic HTML.

📄️ Obi Feeding Robot

People with limited arm mobility use Obi's switch-controlled spoon to eat independently when no caregiver is present.

📄️ Operation Rebound

Southerners with disabilities cite NDS research to campaign for Medicaid expansion and voting-access bills.

📄️ OXO Good Grips

Kitchenware engineers adopt OXO's universal-design handles so new utensils ship grip-friendly for arthritic users.

📄️ Peter Farrelly

Film crews cite Farrelly's push for authentic disability casting to hire disabled actors before scenes are shot.

📄️ PRC-Saltillo

Non-speaking users purchase PRC-Saltillo speech-generating devices to talk when oral speech is impossible.

📄️ Primark

Clothing teams follow Primark's adaptive fasteners guide so new garments launch dress-friendly for disabled shoppers.

📄️ Procter & Gamble

Brand managers add tactile markers and “Easy-Open” lids championed by Procter & Gamble's Sam Latif before items hit shelves.

📄️ Q' Straint

Wheelchair riders use buses fitted with Q' Straint QUANTUM to self-secure when transit lacks staff help.

📄️ QL Plus

Engineering students prototype custom prosthetics for injured veterans through QL Plus capstone projects.

📄️ Rangam Consultants

Autistic job-seekers register with Rangam to land roles in firms that overlook them in mainstream hiring.

📄️ Rifton Equipment

Therapists order Rifton adaptive chairs and gait trainers when school furniture isn't accessible.

📄️ Rodrigo Mendes Institute

Brazilian teachers download the Institute's DIVERSA resources to design inclusive lessons before class.

📄️ Salesforce

Dev teams follow Catherine Nichols' internal a11y checklist so every new cloud feature meets WCAG before release.

📄️ Signup Media

Deaf viewers install the SignUp Chrome extension to see ASL interpretation on Netflix when captions aren't enough.

📄️ So Every BODY Can Move

Amputees lobby statehouses with this campaign to require insurance coverage for sports prosthetics.

📄️ Sony

Gamers test Sony's Access Controller kit so PS5 titles ship with button layouts usable by players with limited dexterity.

📄️ Sorenson Communications

Deaf callers place free ASL video relay calls through Sorenson when businesses have no direct sign-language line.

📄️ Spaulding Rehabilitation

Patients join Spaulding's adaptive rowing and cycling clinics during rehab when local gyms lack inclusive programs.

📄️ Special Olympics

Athletes with intellectual disabilities train and compete in Special Olympics when scholastic leagues exclude them.

📄️ Specialisterne Global

Corporations partner with Specialisterne to redesign hiring tests and onboard autistic talent before job offers go out.

📄️ Spectrum

Customers with disabilities call Spectrum's specialist team or enroll in low-cost “Internet Assist” plans after standard support falls short.

📄️ Spinalpedia

New spinal-cord-injury survivors browse SPINALpedia videos to learn hacks for driving, cooking, or transferring when OT sessions end.

📄️ Squirmy and Grubs

Viewers share Hannah Aylward & Shane Burcaw's videos to pressure publishers and producers to cast real disabled talent before projects start.

📄️ Sunrise Entertainment

Brands hire blind creator Lucy Edwards to audit ad scripts so disability representation is baked in at concept stage.

📄️ Tobii Dynavox

Non-speaking users buy TD I-110 or eye-gaze devices to communicate when mainstream tech lacks AAC.

📄️ Trexo Robotics

Parents lease Trexo exoskeletons so children with CP can practise gait at home when PT hours end.

📄️ United Spinal Association Accessibility Services

Architects hire Dominic Marinelli's team to review blueprints so buildings open ADA-compliant.

📄️ University of Illinois

Companies donate compute to Mark Hasegawa-Johnson's dataset so future voice AIs recognise diverse speech.

📄️ University of Rochester

Pediatric clinics cite Wyatte Hall's research to add ASL exposure guidelines before deaf infants start school.

📄️ U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Committee

Athletes train under USOPC Para programs when hometown gyms lack elite adaptive coaching.

📄️ Verizon Communications

Product squads demo apps in Fred Moltz's lab to fix a11y bugs before apps hit Verizon's stores.

📄️ Vispero

Blind users install JAWS or ZoomText to navigate sites that ignore semantic HTML.

📄️ Walmart

Merch teams follow Victor Calise's policy that every new digital feature must pass internal WCAG audits pre-launch.

📄️ Warrior Games

Wounded service-members compete in Warrior Games when conventional leagues exclude them post-injury.

📄️ We Hear You

Wheelchair users stick the HERO opener on doors and trigger them via the Push app when buildings lack power doors.

📄️ WeWalk

Blind travellers pair the WeWALK cane with GPS to detect obstacles when city infrastructure isn't tactile.

📄️ WeWalk

Wheelchair users book hotels and tours on Wheel the World when mainstream travel sites omit accessibility details.

📄️ Whispp

People who stutter route phone audio through Whispp to convert whispered speech into fluent-sounding voice in real time.

📄️ World Bank

Borrowing countries align projects with the Bank's disability inclusion guidance before loans close.

📄️ World Institute on Disability

Disaster-impacted communities use WID toolkits to demand inclusive emergency plans after inaccessible responses fail them.
